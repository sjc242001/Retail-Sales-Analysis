# Retail Sales Analysis using SQL

### Project Overview

The Retail Sales Analysis project was developed to demonstrate end-to-end SQL-based data analytics capabilities, including database design, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and business intelligence reporting. The project focused on analyzing retail transaction data to uncover customer purchasing behavior, product category performance, sales trends, and operational insights that can support strategic business decision-making.

### Objective

The primary objective was to build a structured retail sales database and leverage SQL to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights. The analysis aimed to identify sales patterns, understand customer demographics, evaluate category-wise performance, and discover opportunities for revenue growth.

### Data Preparation & Database Design

* Designed and created a relational database named **p1_retail_db** to store retail sales transactions.
* Developed a structured table schema containing transaction details, customer demographics, product categories, pricing information, quantity sold, cost of goods sold (COGS), and total sales.
* Performed data quality checks to identify missing, null, and inconsistent records.
* Cleaned the dataset by removing incomplete entries and validating data integrity to ensure reliable analysis.

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

* Assessed the overall dataset by analyzing transaction volume, customer count, and product category distribution.
* Identified unique customers and evaluated purchasing activity across multiple product categories.
* Investigated high-value transactions and analyzed customer demographics such as age and gender to understand purchasing trends.
* Examined sales activity across different time periods to uncover seasonal and monthly performance variations.

### Business Analysis

Using SQL queries, several key business questions were answered:

* Evaluated category-wise sales performance and total revenue contribution.
* Analyzed customer purchasing behavior based on demographics and product preferences.
* Identified top-performing customers based on total sales value.
* Determined the most profitable sales periods through monthly trend analysis.
* Measured transaction distribution across different time shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* Assessed product category popularity and customer engagement levels.
* Investigated high-value transactions to understand premium purchase patterns.

### SQL Concepts Applied

* Aggregate Functions (SUM, AVG, COUNT)
* GROUP BY and ORDER BY Clauses
* DISTINCT Operations
* Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
* Window Functions (RANK)
* Date and Time Functions
* Filtering and Conditional Logic
* Data Cleaning Queries
* Customer Segmentation Analysis

### Key Insights

* Certain product categories consistently generated higher sales and order volumes.
* A small group of customers contributed a significant share of total revenue, indicating high-value customer segments.
* Monthly sales trends revealed peak business periods and seasonal purchasing behavior.
* Customer demographics influenced purchasing patterns across product categories.
* Evening and afternoon shifts recorded higher transaction volumes compared to morning sales.

### Outcome

The project successfully demonstrated the ability to design databases, clean and transform data, write complex SQL queries, and generate actionable business insights. The findings can support retail organizations in improving customer targeting, inventory planning, sales forecasting, and overall business performance.
